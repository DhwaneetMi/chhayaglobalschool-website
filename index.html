Questions Akola parents ask us

Is there any donation or deposit?

No. Never. You pay only the published fees, and you see the full fee details before you decide.

Till which class can my child study here?

Admissions for 2027-28 are open from Playgroup to Std 10, all on one campus in Ramdaspeth.

What happens after Sr KG?

Your child moves straight to Std 1 here, with teachers who already know them. There's no new school to search for and no admission race.

Can I see the school before applying?

Yes, please do. Book a free visit on WhatsApp, bring your child, and meet the teachers.

How do I get the fee details?

Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll share the 2027-28 fee sheet straight away.

Is there an entrance exam?

Not for Playgroup to Sr KG. It's a friendly chat with parents and child. For Std 2 and above, a simple level check helps us place your child well.
